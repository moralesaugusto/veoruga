# Privacy Policy — Veoruga

_Last updated: 2026-08-10_

Veoruga is designed to do one thing and to do it entirely on your device.

## What Veoruga does

- Captures the visible area of the current tab, or a region you drag, when you
  ask it to.
- Lets you download the image as a PNG or copy it to your clipboard.

## Data collection

**Veoruga collects nothing.** Specifically:

- No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted.
- No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising.
- Screenshots never leave your computer — they are created in the page/popup,
  handed to you as a download or clipboard copy, and then discarded. There is no
  server and no network request of any kind.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- **activeTab** — grants Veoruga temporary access to the tab **only** at the
  moment you click the toolbar icon, so it can read the pixels of that page to
  capture them.
- **scripting** — used to inject the on-page selection overlay when you snip a
  region.

Veoruga requests no host permissions, no storage, and no network access.
